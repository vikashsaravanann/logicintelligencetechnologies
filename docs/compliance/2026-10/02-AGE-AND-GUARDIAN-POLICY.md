# Corporate Platform — Age & Guardian Policy
**Project:** `logicintelligencetechnologies` | **Date:** 2026-10-11

## 1. User Age Governance
- **Commercial Account Gate:** Terms of Service require users to be at least 18 years old or legal age of majority to register an account.
- **Pediatric & Clinical Carve-Out:** In clinical contexts, minor patients are treated via authorized parent/guardian proxy. Medical emergency care is NEVER blocked by an age gate.
- **Child Protection Standard:** Adheres to COPPA and UK Age Appropriate Design Code principles; zero profiling or targeted behavioral advertising directed at minors.
