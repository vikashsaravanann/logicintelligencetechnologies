# Corporate Platform — Statutory Applicability & Legal Sources
**Project:** `logicintelligencetechnologies` | **Date:** 2026-10-11

## 1. Applicable Regulatory Regimes
1. **India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023):** Mandates purpose limitation, explicit notice, parental consent for children, and robust security safeguards.
2. **EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) & UK GDPR:** Cross-border transfer mechanisms, lawful basis, data subject access rights, and cookie consent rules.
3. **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) & CIPA:** Prohibits unauthorized wiretapping, session recording without consent, and deceptive cancellation flows.
4. **US CAN-SPAM, Canadian CASL, UK PECR, Australian Spam Act:** Commercial email compliance, truthful headers, clear sender identification, and one-click opt-out.
5. **US DMCA (17 U.S.C. § 512):** Notice-and-takedown safe harbor mechanics for online service providers.
