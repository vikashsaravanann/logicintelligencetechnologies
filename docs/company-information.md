## 5. Claims and Governance Standards

### Defensible Language:
- Focus on capability descriptions, architectural pipelines, configurable risk signals, and evidence generation.
- Clearly identify planned capabilities or capabilities under active development as roadmaps or directions.

### Strictly Forbidden Claims:
- **NO** claims of 100% fraud detection, 100% security, or guaranteed detection.
- **NO** claims of zero false positives or zero data retention unless legally documented.
- **NO** fabricated quantitative metrics (e.g., "99.4% accuracy", "<0.01% false alarms").
- **NO** unverified certifications (e.g., ISO, SOC-2, CERT-In) unless official proof is provided.
- **NO** fabricated integrations (e.g., calendar, banking, messaging) that are not implemented.
