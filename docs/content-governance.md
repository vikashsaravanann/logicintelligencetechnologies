# Content Governance & Anti-Manipulation Policy

## 1. Zero-Fake-Claims Mandate
Logic Intelligence Technologies adheres to a strict policy of factual representation. No marketing material, internal documentation, or SEO strategy may employ fabricated claims, inflated metrics, or false statements.

## 2. Anti-Manipulation Guidelines
- **No Keyword Stuffing:** Content must be human-readable and contextually relevant.
- **No Cloaking:** Search engines and AI crawlers must see the exact same content as human visitors.
- **Entity Integrity:** VoiceShield must ALWAYS be referenced as a product of LIT, never as a standalone corporate entity.

## 3. AI-Safe Documentation
All public-facing documentation must be structured clearly (using semantic HTML and Schema.org markup) to prevent LLM hallucinations during data ingestion. Only facts outlined in the Master AI Knowledge Pack are permissible as ground truth.
